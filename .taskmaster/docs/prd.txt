# Sonar PRD v2: Voice Trading Tutor for Blind and Low-Vision Learners

Technica 2026 (UMD) · Version 2 · October 17, 2026
Supersedes PRD v1. Adds the shipped UI design (space bar, Try asking tray, numbered Gemini options menu, Repeat voice), the voice stack decision, the full lesson curriculum, and the realistic palm and arm visualization. Written in the RPG format so `task-master parse-prd` can build a dependency-aware task graph.

---

<overview>

## Problem Statement

- Investing tools are built around charts. A screen reader turns a chart into a list of numbers, which hides the shape: trends, dips, and recoveries.
- Blind and low-vision people have no safe, accessible place to practice investing and build confidence.
- New ideas like tokenized funds are explained with diagrams, which shuts this audience out of understanding them.
- Existing assistants (ChatGPT, Google, brokerage apps) answer with text and images, invent numbers, and never let a learner feel a chart or practice a trade safely.

## Product Summary

Sonar is a voice-first investing tutor. The learner holds the space bar to talk, picks numbered options read aloud by the tutor, and practices with $10,000 of simulated money. Every number comes from real market data through a tool call. A small robotic arm draws each price chart on the learner's palm (or plays it as rising and falling tones), and a Solana devnet token, PVOO, mirrors the VOO S&P 500 ETF to teach how tokenized funds work. No real money moves.

## Target Users

- **Primary: Maya, 34, blind since birth.** Uses NVDA or VoiceOver daily, types fast, has never invested. Wants to understand what an ETF is and try a trade without risking money or depending on a sighted helper.
- **Secondary: Ray, 61, low vision from macular degeneration.** Sees large, high-contrast shapes. Lost his sight later in life and does not read braille. Wants big text and voice.
- **Tertiary:** any beginner who prefers learning by voice.

## Goals

- A blind learner goes from "what is an ETF?" to a confirmed practice trade, a felt chart, and a backtest without needing a screen.
- Every number Sonar says comes from a tool call, never from the model's memory.
- Teach tokenization honestly: benefits (fast settlement, public record) and risks (trust in the issuer, custody, regulation).
- A modular trading engine where new asset types (options, more stocks, crypto) plug in without changing existing code.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Real-money trading or connecting a real brokerage account.
- Personalized financial advice.
- Native mobile app, sign-up, or multi-user accounts.

## Success Metrics

- A tester with eyes closed completes the full demo flow (lesson, question, felt chart, backtest, trade, backing check, portfolio) with zero sighted help.
- 0 trades execute without a spoken or typed "confirm".
- PVOO on-chain supply equals the ledger's PVOO total after every trade (100% of trades).
- 100% of spoken figures match the database during the scripted test run.
- Spoken reply begins within 4 seconds of releasing the space bar (p90).
- All 5 core lessons playable end to end, each with a passing quiz.

</overview>

<functional-decomposition>

## Capability Tree

### Capability: Voice Interaction
Turns the learner's voice into text and the tutor's text into a warm, human voice.

#### Feature: Push-to-talk capture
- **Description**: Record microphone audio while the space bar (or the on-screen key) is held.
- **Inputs**: keydown and keyup of Space, pointer down and up on the key.
- **Outputs**: audio blob (webm/opus) and a "listening" state event.
- **Behavior**: ignore key repeat; minimum hold of 250 ms; play a rising earcon on press and a falling earcon on release; cancel on Escape.

#### Feature: Speech-to-text
- **Description**: Transcribe the recorded clip.
- **Inputs**: audio blob.
- **Outputs**: transcript string plus confidence.
- **Behavior**: send to ElevenLabs Speech-to-Text through the server route; if confidence is low or the call fails, say "I didn't catch that, try again" and keep the typing fallback open.

#### Feature: Text-to-speech
- **Description**: Speak every tutor reply in a consistent, human voice.
- **Inputs**: reply text, voice id, rate setting.
- **Outputs**: streamed audio played in the browser.
- **Behavior**: ElevenLabs streaming TTS is primary. Gemini TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`, style prompt "warm, patient teacher, slightly slow") is the automatic fallback. Browser speechSynthesis is the last resort. Starting a new utterance cancels the old one.

#### Feature: Repeat voice
- **Description**: Replay the last thing the tutor said.
- **Inputs**: R key or the Repeat voice button.
- **Outputs**: the cached audio replayed; if an answer has a chart, the chart redraws too.
- **Behavior**: cache the last audio buffer so repeat is instant and costs no API call.

#### Feature: Typing fallback
- **Description**: Let the learner type a question instead of speaking.
- **Inputs**: T key opens a text field; Enter sends.
- **Outputs**: transcript string into the same pipeline as speech.
- **Behavior**: the field is labeled for screen readers and closes after sending.

#### Feature: Audio cues
- **Description**: Short non-speech sounds for state changes.
- **Inputs**: state events.
- **Outputs**: earcons for listening start, listening end, success chime, wrong-answer tone, trade confirmed thump.
- **Behavior**: generated with the Web Audio API, no sound files.

### Capability: Accessible Interface
The single-screen web app described in the UI specification (see Appendix A).

#### Feature: Space bar hero
- **Description**: A giant glass space bar that is the main control.
- **Inputs**: Space key, pointer.
- **Outputs**: visual press-down, glow, soundwave inside the key, state change to listening.
- **Behavior**: the heading above it always tells the learner what to do next ("Press space to talk", "Press N for the next step").

#### Feature: Try asking tray
- **Description**: A glass panel that slides up from the bottom while the space bar is held.
- **Inputs**: listening state, number keys 1 to 9, clicks.
- **Outputs**: chosen suggestion sent on release.
- **Behavior**: two groups. Lessons (1 to 5) show "Done" and "Up next" tags. Quick questions (6 to 9). The first time it opens, the tutor reads two suggestions aloud.

#### Feature: Numbered options menu
- **Description**: The tutor offers numbered options, listed vertically under the space bar and read aloud.
- **Inputs**: number keys 0 to 9, M key, Escape, menu definitions from the tutor.
- **Outputs**: navigation to lessons, questions, practice, settings, help.
- **Behavior**: opens on launch and on M. Main menu: 1 Lessons, 2 Ask a question, 3 Practice trading, 4 Settings, 5 Help. Submenus add "0 Back". Gemini may add one context-aware suggestion (for example "Continue Stocks vs. ETFs") through the `suggest_options` tool, labeled "Options from Gemini".

#### Feature: Answer card
- **Description**: A glass card with the realistic palm visualization, legend, and large caption.
- **Inputs**: reply text, chart ops.
- **Outputs**: the caption, the animated drawing, the "Drawing on your palm" status.
- **Behavior**: hides the canvas when an answer has no chart; legend uses solid versus dashed lines, never color alone.

#### Feature: Bottom dock
- **Description**: Two always-present buttons: M Menu and R Repeat voice.
- **Inputs**: clicks, M and R keys.
- **Outputs**: opens the main menu or repeats the last utterance.
- **Behavior**: hidden only while the Try asking tray is open.

#### Feature: Settings
- **Description**: Numbered settings menu.
- **Inputs**: number keys.
- **Outputs**: updated, persisted settings and a spoken confirmation ("Speaking speed is now fast").
- **Behavior**: speaking speed (slow, normal, fast), chart sounds (on or off), text size (standard, large, extra large), palm arm (connect or disconnect), reset lesson progress.

#### Feature: Screen reader support
- **Description**: Full use with NVDA and VoiceOver.
- **Inputs**: all UI state.
- **Outputs**: aria-live announcements, labeled controls, focus kept on the space bar.
- **Behavior**: every caption is mirrored to a polite live region; the canvas has an alt description per drawing; no information is conveyed by color alone.

### Capability: Tutor Brain
Gemini function-calling loop that decides what to say and which tools to call.

#### Feature: Function-calling loop
- **Description**: Turn a learner utterance into a spoken reply plus side effects.
- **Inputs**: transcript, conversation history, current app state (menu, lesson, pending trade).
- **Outputs**: reply text, tool results, UI directives (show chart, open menu, start lesson).
- **Behavior**: up to 5 tool rounds per turn; numbers in the reply must come from tool results in the same turn.

#### Feature: Tool registry
- **Description**: The 11 core tools from v1 plus 5 tutoring tools.
- **Inputs**: tool name and JSON arguments.
- **Outputs**: JSON results.
- **Behavior**: core: `list_markets`, `get_quote`, `get_price_history`, `show_chart`, `propose_trade`, `confirm_trade`, `cancel_trade`, `get_portfolio`, `run_backtest`, `review_trades`, `check_token_backing`. Tutoring: `start_lesson`, `next_step`, `answer_quiz`, `suggest_options`, `explain_fees`.

#### Feature: Speech style filter
- **Description**: Make replies sound natural when spoken.
- **Inputs**: raw model text.
- **Outputs**: speakable text.
- **Behavior**: strip markdown; split sentences longer than 25 words; speak numbers naturally ("about 3.5 percent", "five hundred sixty dollars"); describe shapes, never colors; append a one-line risk note whenever a return is mentioned.

#### Feature: Guardrails
- **Description**: Keep the tutor accurate and safe.
- **Inputs**: model output, tool log.
- **Outputs**: approved reply or a corrected reply.
- **Behavior**: reject any reply containing a price or percent that is not in this turn's tool results; refuse personalized advice ("Sonar teaches, it doesn't recommend"); never claim a trade happened without `confirm_trade` succeeding.

### Capability: Lesson Engine
Plays the scripted curriculum in Appendix B.

#### Feature: Curriculum loader
- **Description**: Load lessons from JSON files.
- **Inputs**: `lessons/*.json`.
- **Outputs**: validated lesson objects (id, title, steps, quiz).
- **Behavior**: schema-validate on startup; placeholders like `{AAPL.return_1m}` are filled by tool calls at play time.

#### Feature: Step player
- **Description**: Play one step: narration, chart, arm drawing, all in sync.
- **Inputs**: lesson id, step index.
- **Outputs**: spoken narration, chart ops to the palm and the arm.
- **Behavior**: resolves data placeholders, starts TTS and the drawing together, ends with "Press N for the next step, or R to repeat". N or right arrow advances, P or left arrow goes back.

#### Feature: Quiz engine
- **Description**: One multiple-choice check at the end of each lesson.
- **Inputs**: number key 1 to 3 or a spoken answer.
- **Outputs**: correct or not, explanation, progress update.
- **Behavior**: correct gives a chime and a double palm tap, then offers the next lesson; wrong explains why and lets the learner retry.

#### Feature: Progress store
- **Description**: Remember completed lessons and the next lesson.
- **Inputs**: quiz results.
- **Outputs**: progress map; "Up next" lesson.
- **Behavior**: stored in the database for the demo user and mirrored to localStorage.

### Capability: Trading Engine
Standalone simulated brokerage with a REST API. Knows nothing about Gemini, voice, or the arm.

#### Feature: Market registry
- **Description**: Plug-in list of tradable assets.
- **Inputs**: market modules.
- **Outputs**: assets VOO, SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, PVOO (and stretch: options).
- **Behavior**: each market implements `quote`, `fill`, `settle`; adding a market touches no other code.

#### Feature: Ledger
- **Description**: Cash and positions for the practice account.
- **Inputs**: fills.
- **Outputs**: balances, positions, trade history.
- **Behavior**: starts at $10,000 cash; double-entry rows; never negative cash.

#### Feature: Two-step trades
- **Description**: Propose, then confirm, enforced in backend code.
- **Inputs**: side, symbol, quantity; then proposal id plus confirm.
- **Outputs**: a proposal (price, total, risk note, expiry 60 s), then a fill.
- **Behavior**: `confirm_trade` fails unless a live proposal exists; weekend fills use the last stored close and say so.

#### Feature: Portfolio and coaching
- **Description**: Report holdings and give one habit tip.
- **Inputs**: ledger, trade history.
- **Outputs**: value, gain or loss, one coaching tip every 3 trades.
- **Behavior**: tips are rule-based (concentration over 50%, frequent trading, selling after a dip).

#### Feature: Backtest
- **Description**: "What if I had invested $1,000 a year ago?"
- **Inputs**: symbol, amount, start date.
- **Outputs**: end value, return, maximum drawdown and its date.
- **Behavior**: uses daily closes from Tiger Data.

### Capability: Market Data
Prices stored and summarized in Tiger Data.

#### Feature: Price ingest
- **Description**: Load daily and minute prices.
- **Inputs**: Alpaca market data API; CSV backup files.
- **Outputs**: rows in the `prices` hypertable.
- **Behavior**: backfill 2 years of daily bars for 6 symbols; refresh every 15 minutes during market hours.

#### Feature: Continuous aggregates
- **Description**: Weekly and monthly candles computed by the database.
- **Inputs**: `prices`.
- **Outputs**: `candles_weekly`, `candles_monthly`.
- **Behavior**: refresh policies every hour; tutor tools read aggregates, never raw ticks.

#### Feature: Chart series pipeline
- **Description**: Turn prices into a palm-sized drawing.
- **Inputs**: closes for a range.
- **Outputs**: 12 to 16 normalized points plus key markers (peak, trough, end).
- **Behavior**: SMA (5-day for 1 month, 20-day for 6 months) then Ramer-Douglas-Peucker down to about 12 points; normalize to the drawing box; shared scale when two series are compared.

#### Feature: CSV fallback
- **Description**: Keep working if the database is unreachable.
- **Inputs**: `data/fallback/*.csv`.
- **Outputs**: same shapes as the database queries.
- **Behavior**: automatic on connection error; logged.

### Capability: Tokenized Fund Settlement
PVOO, a practice token on Solana devnet that mirrors one VOO share.

#### Feature: Mint and burn
- **Description**: Mint PVOO on buy, burn on sell.
- **Inputs**: confirmed trade.
- **Outputs**: transaction signature, settlement time in seconds, Explorer link.
- **Behavior**: SPL token with 6 decimals; custodial devnet key held by the chain service.

#### Feature: Backing check
- **Description**: Prove tokens are backed.
- **Inputs**: none.
- **Outputs**: on-chain supply, ledger total, match yes or no.
- **Behavior**: compares `getTokenSupply` with the ledger; spoken as "Supply matches the ledger: 2 tokens on chain, 2 in your account".

#### Feature: Ledger-only fallback
- **Description**: Keep PVOO trades working if devnet is down.
- **Inputs**: RPC errors or timeouts over 8 s.
- **Outputs**: ledger fill marked "simulated settlement".
- **Behavior**: the tutor says so out loud and shows an earlier Explorer link as an example.

### Capability: Chart Output
Makes every chart perceivable without sight.

#### Feature: Sonification
- **Description**: Play a chart as sound.
- **Inputs**: chart ops.
- **Outputs**: tones from 220 Hz (low) to 880 Hz (high), a click per point, a low thump per tap.
- **Behavior**: always available with S; automatic when the arm is disconnected or "Chart sounds" is on.

#### Feature: Realistic palm visualization
- **Description**: On-screen model of the hand and the arm, for sighted judges and helpers.
- **Inputs**: chart ops, arm telemetry when connected.
- **Outputs**: animated canvas.
- **Behavior**: a shaded, realistic palm with creases; a two-joint arm with lift servo solved by inverse kinematics; the stylus lifts between strokes, presses down to draw, and leaves a soft glowing trace; eased motion with acceleration limits; idle breathing sway. Details in Appendix A.4.

#### Feature: Arm path planner
- **Description**: Convert chart ops into arm commands.
- **Inputs**: normalized points, taps, pauses.
- **Outputs**: serial commands (`P` pen, `M` move, `T` tap, `W` wait, `H` home).
- **Behavior**: maps the drawing box to a 6 x 4 cm area on the palm; caps speed at 4 cm/s.

### Capability: Palm Arm Hardware
The physical device.

#### Feature: Firmware
- **Description**: ESP32 firmware driving 2 joint servos and 1 lift servo.
- **Inputs**: serial commands at 115200 baud.
- **Outputs**: motion, `OK` and `ERR` acks.
- **Behavior**: inverse kinematics on the device; trapezoidal velocity profile; soft limits on angles and speed.

#### Feature: Safety
- **Description**: The learner can always pull away and nothing presses hard.
- **Inputs**: lift servo position, optional force sensor (stretch).
- **Outputs**: stylus lifts automatically on timeout or high force.
- **Behavior**: soft inkless stylus; maximum pressure level 5 of 5 enforced in firmware; auto-lift after 3 s of no commands.

## Module Definitions

### Module: voice
- **Maps to capability**: Voice Interaction
- **Responsibility**: microphone in, human voice out
- **Exports**: `startCapture()`, `stopCapture(): Blob`, `transcribe(blob)`, `speak(text, opts)`, `repeatLast()`, `earcon(name)`

### Module: ui
- **Maps to capability**: Accessible Interface
- **Responsibility**: render states and route keys
- **Exports**: `<SpaceBar/>`, `<TryAskingTray/>`, `<OptionsMenu/>`, `<AnswerCard/>`, `<BottomDock/>`, `useKeymap()`, `announce(text)`

### Module: tutor
- **Maps to capability**: Tutor Brain
- **Responsibility**: Gemini loop, tools, speech style, guardrails
- **Exports**: `runTurn(transcript, state)`, `registerTool(def)`, `toSpeech(text)`, `checkReply(reply, toolLog)`

### Module: lessons
- **Maps to capability**: Lesson Engine
- **Responsibility**: load, play, quiz, track progress
- **Exports**: `loadCurriculum()`, `playStep(lessonId, i)`, `gradeQuiz(lessonId, choice)`, `getProgress()`, `nextLesson()`

### Module: engine
- **Maps to capability**: Trading Engine
- **Responsibility**: practice brokerage over REST
- **Exports**: REST endpoints listed above; Python `Market` base class

### Module: data
- **Maps to capability**: Market Data
- **Responsibility**: prices, aggregates, chart series
- **Exports**: `get_quote(sym)`, `get_history(sym, range)`, `chart_series(sym, range, points=12)`, `compare_series(a, b, range)`

### Module: chain
- **Maps to capability**: Tokenized Fund Settlement
- **Responsibility**: PVOO mint, burn, supply
- **Exports**: `POST /mint`, `POST /burn`, `GET /supply`

### Module: palm
- **Maps to capability**: Chart Output
- **Responsibility**: on-screen palm and arm model, sonification
- **Exports**: `PalmScene.draw(ops)`, `PalmScene.tap(at)`, `sonify(ops)`

### Module: bridge
- **Maps to capability**: Chart Output (arm path planner)
- **Responsibility**: chart ops to serial commands
- **Exports**: `plan(ops): string[]`, `connectArm()`, `send(cmds)`

### Module: firmware
- **Maps to capability**: Palm Arm Hardware
- **Responsibility**: safe motion
- **Exports**: serial protocol (Appendix C)

<test-strategy>

## Test Pyramid

```
        /\
       /E2E\       ← 15% (Playwright keyboard-only flows, one eyes-closed manual run)
      /------\
     /Integration\ ← 35% (engine + DB, chain on devnet, tutor loop with recorded tools)
    /------------\
   /  Unit Tests  \ ← 50% (series math, guardrails, state machine, quiz grading, planner)
  /----------------\
```

## Coverage Requirements
- Line coverage: 70% minimum for services/engine, services/tutor, services/lessons
- Branch coverage: 60% minimum
- Function coverage: 75% minimum
- Statement coverage: 70% minimum
- UI and firmware are covered by e2e and bench tests instead of line coverage.

## Critical Test Scenarios

### Two-step trades
**Happy path**: propose buy 2 PVOO, confirm. Expected: fill, mint, supply equals ledger.
**Edge cases**: confirm after 60 s. Expected: "That offer expired, want me to quote it again?"
**Error cases**: confirm with no proposal. Expected: 409 and the tutor does not claim success.
**Integration points**: devnet down. Expected: ledger-only fill, spoken notice.

### Guardrails
**Happy path**: reply numbers all appear in the tool log. Expected: passes.
**Error cases**: reply contains "up 12 percent" with no tool result. Expected: blocked and regenerated.
**Edge cases**: "Should I buy NVDA?" Expected: education-only answer, no recommendation.

### Chart series
**Happy path**: 22 closes in, 12 points out, peak preserved.
**Edge cases**: flat series. Expected: a centered flat line, narration says "barely moved".
**Integration points**: compare mode uses one shared scale for both series.

### Lesson engine
**Happy path**: lesson plays all steps and the quiz passes. Expected: progress saved, "Up next" moves.
**Error cases**: a placeholder tool fails. Expected: the step speaks without the number and says the data is unavailable.

### Interface
**Happy path**: launch, hear the menu, press 1, 1, play lesson 1 with N, answer 2. Expected: chime and double tap.
**Edge cases**: press R before anything was said. Expected: repeats the welcome.
**Accessibility**: NVDA reads each caption once through the live region; focus stays on the space bar.

## Test Generation Guidelines
- Prefer deterministic fixtures: seeded price series and recorded Gemini tool calls.
- Never hit real Alpaca or Gemini in unit tests; devnet only in tagged integration tests.
- E2E tests drive the app with the keyboard only, as a blind user would.

</test-strategy>

## System Components

1. **Learner**: speaks, feels palm charts, hears tones.
2. **Interface** (apps/web): the single-screen app, push-to-talk, ElevenLabs voice.
3. **Tutor** (services/tutor + services/lessons): prompts, Gemini function-calling loop, tool registry, lesson engine.
4. **Trading engine** (services/engine): markets, ledger, settlement over REST.
5. **Services and hardware**: Tiger Data, Alpaca with CSV backup, Solana devnet chain service, palm arm over Web Serial.

Each layer talks only to its neighbor.

## Data Models

```sql
CREATE TABLE prices (ts TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, symbol TEXT NOT NULL, open NUMERIC, high NUMERIC, low NUMERIC, close NUMERIC, volume BIGINT);
SELECT create_hypertable('prices', 'ts');
CREATE MATERIALIZED VIEW candles_weekly WITH (timescaledb.continuous) AS
  SELECT time_bucket('7 days', ts) AS bucket, symbol, first(open, ts) AS open, max(high) AS high, min(low) AS low, last(close, ts) AS close
  FROM prices GROUP BY bucket, symbol;
-- candles_monthly: same with '30 days'
CREATE TABLE trades (id UUID PRIMARY KEY, ts TIMESTAMPTZ, side TEXT, symbol TEXT, qty NUMERIC, price NUMERIC, status TEXT, tx_sig TEXT, settle_ms INT);
CREATE TABLE positions (symbol TEXT PRIMARY KEY, qty NUMERIC, avg_cost NUMERIC);
CREATE TABLE cash (id INT PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT 1, balance NUMERIC NOT NULL CHECK (balance >= 0));
```

## Technology Stack

- Web: Next.js 15, TypeScript, Canvas 2D for the palm scene, Web Audio, Web Serial.
- Tutor: Gemini API with function calling (Flash model; exact ID to confirm on the day).
- Voice: ElevenLabs STT and streaming TTS; Gemini TTS fallback; browser speech last resort.
- Engine and data: Python 3.12, FastAPI, psycopg, Tiger Data (Postgres + TimescaleDB).
- Chain: Node 20, @solana/web3.js v1, @solana/spl-token, devnet RPC.
- Hardware: ESP32, 3 x SG90 or MG90S servos, soft inkless stylus.

**Decision: Voice stack (ElevenLabs primary, Gemini TTS fallback)**
- **Rationale**: ElevenLabs gives the most natural voice and qualifies for the ElevenLabs prize. Gemini TTS (30 prebuilt voices, style prompts, streaming) is a strong backup and keeps the Gemini prize in play.
- **Trade-offs**: two vendors to configure; fallback voice sounds different.
- **Alternatives considered**: Gemini Live API (real-time speech in and out with function calling, most humanlike) as a stretch goal if time remains; browser speechSynthesis rejected as primary because it sounds robotic.

**Decision: Single dark visual theme**
- **Rationale**: bright text on deep color reads best for low vision; one theme halves the design work.
- **Trade-offs**: no light mode.
- **Alternatives considered**: light and dark themes with high-contrast mode (prototyped, judged too complex).

**Decision: Engine in Python, chain in Node**
- **Rationale**: pandas-friendly backtests; Solana's best SDK is JavaScript.
- **Trade-offs**: two runtimes.
- **Alternatives considered**: all TypeScript (possible if the team prefers one language).

## Appendix B: Lesson Curriculum

Numbers in braces are placeholders filled by tools at play time. Every lesson ends with the line "Press N for the next step, or R to repeat" until the quiz.

### Lesson 1: How to feel a graph (2 min)
Goal: learn the palm's "language".
1. **Left to right**. Say: "Rest your hand palm up. I'll draw a slow line from left to right. Left is the past. Right is today." Draw: flat line across the box.
2. **Up means up**. Say: "Now a line that climbs. When the line moves up, the price went up." Draw: rising diagonal.
3. **The tap**. Say: "Feel that tap? A tap always means something important happened, like a peak or a finished trade." Draw: one tap in the center.
Quiz: "Where on your palm is today?" Options: the left side, the right side, the center. Answer: right side. Why: drawings always move from the past on the left to today on the right.

### Lesson 2: Stocks vs. ETFs (5 min)
Goal: understand diversification and fees.
1. **One stock's trend**. Data: `get_price_history(AAPL, 1m)`. Say: "Here's Apple over the last month, smoothed with a 5-day average. It's {AAPL.dir} about {AAPL.pct} percent. The tap marks its highest point." Draw: AAPL line, tap at the peak.
2. **What an ETF is**. Say: "An ETF is one share that holds many companies. VOO holds the 500 largest US companies, so you own a tiny slice of each." Draw: none (tone chord).
3. **Feel the difference**. Data: AAPL and VOO, 1 month, shared scale. Say: "First Apple on its own, then VOO on the same scale. The second line barely moves. That smoothing is diversification." Draw: AAPL solid, pause, VOO dashed.
4. **Fees add up**. Data: computed by `explain_fees(0.03, 1.0, 30)`. Say: "Two funds with the same returns but different yearly fees, over 30 years. The 1 percent fee leaves you about a quarter less than VOO's 0.03 percent." Draw: two growth curves.
Quiz: "Which usually moves more in a day: one stock, or a broad ETF?" Answer: one stock.

### Lesson 3: Your first practice trade (4 min)
Goal: learn quotes, orders, confirmation, and the portfolio.
1. **Getting a quote**. Data: `get_quote(VOO)`. Say: "VOO last traded at {VOO.price} dollars. That's the price for one share." If the market is closed: "The market is closed, so this is Friday's closing price."
2. **Two steps to buy**. Say: "Buying always takes two steps. First I read back the order. Nothing happens until you press Enter or say confirm." Action: `propose_trade(buy, VOO, 1)`.
3. **Confirm**. Action: `confirm_trade(id)`. Say: "Done. You own one share of VOO with practice money. It settles the next business day." Draw: short line and a tap.
4. **Your portfolio**. Data: `get_portfolio()`. Say: "You have {cash} in practice cash and one VOO share worth {value}." Draw: none.
Quiz: "What has to happen before Sonar places a trade?" Options: you say confirm; nothing, it buys right away; you wait a day. Answer: you say confirm.

### Lesson 4: Tokenized funds with PVOO (6 min)
Goal: understand tokenization, its benefits, and its risks.
1. **What a token is**. Say: "A tokenized fund records your share on a blockchain instead of a broker's private database. PVOO is our practice token. One PVOO stands for one share of VOO."
2. **Settlement speed**. Say: "A normal ETF trade settles the next business day. That's the long line. A token on Solana settles in about 2 seconds. That's the short line and the tap." Draw: long line, short line, tap.
3. **Buy PVOO**. Action: propose then confirm 2 PVOO; mint on devnet. Say: "Done. That settled in {settle_s} seconds. Feel the tap."
4. **Check the backing**. Data: `check_token_backing()`. Say: "Anyone can check this. On chain there are {supply} PVOO, and our ledger says {ledger}. They match, so every token is backed."
5. **The risks**. Say: "Tokens still depend on trust. The issuer has to really hold the shares. Whoever holds the keys controls the tokens. In this app we hold your keys, like a bank would. And the rules for tokenized securities are still being written." Draw: one tap.
Quiz: "If you lose your own wallet keys, can a bank reset them?" Answer: no, the tokens are gone.

### Lesson 5: What if? Backtesting and risk (5 min)
Goal: learn returns, drawdowns, and time in the market.
1. **The question**. Say: "Let's ask: what if you'd put 1,000 dollars into VOO a year ago?"
2. **The result**. Data: `run_backtest(VOO, 1000, 1y)`. Say: "It would be worth {end_value} today, a change of {return_pct} percent." Draw: the year's line, tap at the end.
3. **The worst drop**. Say: "The biggest fall along the way was {max_dd} percent, around {dd_date}. Feel the dip. Holding through drops like this is the hard part." Draw: line with a tap at the trough.
4. **Past is not a promise**. Say: "This tells you what happened, not what will happen. Prices can fall as well as rise."
Quiz: "What does a backtest tell you?" Options: what happened in the past; what will happen next year; which stock to buy. Answer: the past.

### Lesson 6: Options basics (stretch, 6 min)
1. **Buying a call**. Say: "The dashed line is zero. If the stock stays low, you lose only the 2 dollars you paid. Past the strike price, the line climbs. The tap marks break-even." Draw: zero line, hockey stick, tap.
2. **Buying a put**. Mirror image.
3. **Time works against you**. Draw: value decaying faster near expiration. Say that most beginners lose money on options; paper money only.
Quiz: "You paid 2 dollars for a call. The stock crashed. What's the most you can lose?" Answer: 2 dollars.

## Appendix C: Arm Serial Protocol
- `H` home and lift · `P0` lift, `P1` lower · `M x y` move to normalized x, y in [0,1] inside the 6 x 4 cm box · `T ms` tap · `W ms` wait · `F n` set max pressure 1 to 5.
- Device replies `OK` or `ERR code`. Auto-lift after 3 s without a command.
